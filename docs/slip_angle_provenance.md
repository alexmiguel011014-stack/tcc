**Question.** For each of the 5 Wang tracks, does any column equal `atan2(vy, vx)` (rad or deg, ±sign) to within floating-point/rounding tolerance? If yes → SA is *computed* from vx, vy and carries no independent information. If the closest column differs by more than sensor-noise level → SA is *measured* (GPS/INS) and is a legitimate independent third output.

Reading:

- **col 3 `Sideslip_angle` = atan2(vy 10, vx 9) to floating-point precision on 4/5 tracks** (and vy 10 = vx 9·tan(col 3) likewise). The raw slip angle and the raw velocity pair are the *same information* — one was derived from the other inside the GPS/INS post-processing. On wang81 the identity holds up to a constant +0.370 m/s added to vy 10.
- **col 19 `SA_smooth` is a low-pass of col 3** (≈ 21-sample moving average, r > 0.999 on all tracks). It was *not* recomputed from the smooth velocity pair.
- The mismatch between col 19 and atan2(vy 17, vx 18) on wang31 / wang81 / wang32 is a **constant offset in vy_smooth of ≈ +0.30 to +0.37 m/s** (last-but-one column: RMSE ≈ |mean|, r ≈ 1). A constant δ in vy produces a residual ≈ δ / vx, which is the slow, 1/vx-shaped curve in the figures — not sensor noise. Whoever bias-corrected `vy_smooth` on those three tracks did not recompute `SA_smooth`. wang21 / wang22 carry no such offset (≈ 0.001–0.002 m/s).
- `vx_smooth` also differs from the raw `vx` by a track-specific constant (last column), i.e. the *_smooth columns were individually offset-corrected per track.

## Verdict

- **Verdict: computed** — SA column: **19** (`SA_smooth`, rad) for the smoothed signal; **3** (`Sideslip_angle`, deg) raw.
- The raw slip angle is `atan2(vy, vx)` of the raw velocity pair to 1e-16 on 4/5 tracks (wang81: same, after a +0.370 m/s constant on vy). The smooth slip angle is a ~100 ms moving average of the raw one. **Neither column carries information that is not already in (vx, vy).**
- Relative to the IC's own outputs (cols 17, 18) the SA column is *inconsistent* on three of five tracks by a constant vy offset of 0.30–0.37 m/s. Training a 5×3 model on col 19 as-is would force it to learn a per-track sensor artefact that is not predictable from the inputs.

### Consequence for GOALS 3

- Define the third output as **β := atan2(vy 17, vx 18)** (consistent with y1, y2), *not* as col 19. Then arm B (5×3 MIMO) vs arm A (5×2 + post-hoc atan2) tests exactly one hypothesis: whether fitting β jointly regularises the vx/vy structure. Any gain cannot come from new measurements, because there are none.
- Report RMSE_β against that same definition; optionally also against col 19 for wang21/wang22 only (the two tracks where the offset is negligible).
- `(manual)` Ask the advisor / Jilin contact why `vy_smooth` was offset on wang31/81/32 and whether the raw or the smooth pair is the trustworthy reference — this also affects the IC's reported RMSE on those tracks.
